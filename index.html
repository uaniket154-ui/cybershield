UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

Cyber Crime Is Closer Than You Think

Cyber crime includes illegal activities carried out using computers, smartphones, networks or the internet. Understanding common threats helps you identify suspicious activity before it becomes a problem.

COMMON THREATS

Know Your Enemy

Learn about common cyber crimes and how they can affect individuals and organizations.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick people into giving away sensitive information.

⚠ Fake links & messages
💳

Online Fraud

Online scams that attempt to steal money or financial information through deception.

⚠ Fake offers & payments
🪪

Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without permission.

⚠ Personal information
🔑

Password Attacks

Attempts to gain unauthorized access by guessing, reusing or obtaining passwords.

⚠ Weak passwords
💬

Cyberbullying

Harassment, threats or harmful behaviour carried out through digital platforms.

⚠ Harmful online behaviour
🧠

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or performing actions that compromise security.

⚠ Psychological manipulation

STAY PROTECTED

Simple Habits, Stronger Security

01

Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for important accounts and avoid reusing the same password everywhere.

02

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds an additional layer of protection to your accounts.

03

Think Before Clicking

Check links, sender details and website addresses before opening anything suspicious.

04

Keep Software Updated

Install trusted security updates to reduce known security weaknesses.

05

Protect Personal Information

Avoid publicly sharing passwords, financial information, OTPs or sensitive personal details.

06

Verify Before Trusting

If someone urgently asks for money or information, verify their identity using another trusted method.

SCAM WARNING

Watch Out For These Red Flags

!

Urgency

"Act immediately or your account will be closed."

!

Unexpected Links

A message asks you to click a link you were not expecting.

!

Requests for OTPs

Someone asks you to share an OTP, password or PIN.

!

Too Good to Be True

A prize, job or offer seems unrealistically attractive.
