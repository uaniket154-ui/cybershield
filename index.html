01 / AWARENESS

Why Cyber Awareness Matters

Technology connects us with the world, but it also creates opportunities for cybercriminals. Awareness helps us identify suspicious behaviour before it becomes a serious problem.

🌐

Digital World

Students use smartphones, social media, online banking, cloud services and educational platforms every day.

⚠️

Growing Threats

Phishing, scams, malware, identity theft and account compromise can affect ordinary users.

🛡️

Smart Defence

Strong passwords, MFA, software updates and careful online behaviour reduce many common risks.

03 / DIGITAL DEFENCE

Build Your Cyber Defence

Cybersecurity does not always require complicated technology. Small and consistent habits can make a major difference.

✓

Create Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for important accounts.

✓

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Add another verification step to important accounts.

✓

Verify Before Clicking

Check unexpected links, attachments and requests.

✓

Keep Software Updated

Install security updates for devices and applications.

✓

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information online.

🔒 PROTECTED DIGITAL IDENTITY
🚨
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Act Fast. Preserve Evidence.

If you suspect cybercrime, save screenshots, transaction details, emails and messages. Contact your bank or service provider quickly if money or an account is involved.

In India, cybercrime incidents can be reported through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
